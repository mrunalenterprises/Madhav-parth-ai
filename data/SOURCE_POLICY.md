# Source and verification policy

Use one clearly identified Sanskrit edition and 700-verse numbering scheme. Record edition, URL or book reference, acquisition date, rights and provenance. Structural validation is not source verification. A reviewer must compare all 700 texts with the chosen edition and approve each individually.

Modern translations and commentaries may have separate copyright restrictions. Do not publish or import them without permission or an appropriate license. Never fabricate missing verses or silently normalize textual variants.
